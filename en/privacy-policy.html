PRIVACY POLICY 🗿

At The Sigma Games ("we", "our"), accessible via thesigmagames.github.io, user privacy and safety are among our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information processed when you play our games and browse our website.

1. Information Collection & Use

Our platform does not collect, store, or process personal identifiable information such as full names, user accounts, passwords, or payment details.

  • Server Log Files: Hosted via GitHub Pages, standard anonymous access logs (such as IP addresses, browser types, and timestamp data) may be processed for security and network performance by GitHub.
  • Local Storage / Game Saves: Game progression, options, and high scores are saved exclusively inside your device browser's Local Storage. No saved game data is transmitted to remote servers.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We may partner with verified third-party services to deliver advertisements or analyze visitor metrics:

  • Google AdSense: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage metrics may be monitored to help us understand which games are popular and improve site usability.

3. External Web Links

Our website contains links to external platforms such as Chess.com, Scratch, or developer social profiles. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy policies of third-party websites.

4. Children's Privacy

Our games are targeted at a general audience. We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
